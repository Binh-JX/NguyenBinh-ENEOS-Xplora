HOW I THINK

An idea is never the beginning.

A question is. Here is how one moves — from noticing something worth asking about, to a genuine opportunity worth acting on.

01

Observe

Notice a problem, a phenomenon, a contradiction.

02

Question

Why? Why not? What if?

03

Explore

Knowledge, experts, technology scouting — and, increasingly, AI.

04

Challenge

Has this been done? By whom? How mature is it?

05

Reframe

Approached differently. Applied elsewhere. Connected to something else.

06

Opportunity

A genuine technology or R&D opportunity, named.

07

Validate

Feasibility. Novelty. Scalability. Impact.

08

Act

A proposal. A pilot. A collaboration. Sometimes, just a better question.

AI × INNOVATION

AI doesn't replace the thinking.

It expands the space in which I can think — how far I can search, how many ideas I can compare, how fast I can find out whether something has already been done, by whom, and how far it has gone.
